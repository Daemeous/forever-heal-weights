Forever Healer Stat Weights

Stat weights for Priest, Paladin, Shaman and Druid healers, worked out from your own combat log, fight by fight. The log is read in your browser: nothing is uploaded, and the page works offline once loaded.

1. Choose your combat log

Drop WoWCombatLog-….txt here, or click to choose Usually in World of Warcraft\_forever_\Logs\. Advanced Combat Logging must be on (System › Network). Several files are fine.
Download a data sample (for debugging)

Reads the report through the Warcraft Logs API. You sign in to Warcraft Logs once (this page never sees your password), and the report must have been logged with Advanced Combat Logging on.

2. Pick the healer and check the inputs

More inputs

Bonus healing is the input that matters most. It is worked out from your own heals when the log allows; otherwise use your character sheet's figure (plus any Spirit or Intellect your talents turn into healing). Int talents: e.g. Mental Strength / Heart of the Wild 10%. Spirit → healing: Priest Spiritual Guidance, 5% per point. Spirit talents: Druid Living Spirit. Int → healing: Shaman Mental Quickness. Regen while casting: Meditation / Mindfulness / Reflection, usually 50% if taken.

Per encounter

Weights are healing gained per point of each stat, relative to +1 bonus healing (= 1.00). Short fights are noisy: compare the boss-fight totals above before acting on a single pull.

Spells

Healing per mana uses what each spell really cost you (after talents); healing a spell triggers without a cast of its own (Prayer of Mending bounces, Bloom, Holy Binding) is credited to it, not to the spell you cast to trigger it. Shield absorbs count as healing.

Best in bags

Paste the export from the MythicSim Forever or voidsim.io addon (the text it asks you to copy). It lists what you wear and what is in your bags; the best gear you own for your weights (the view selected above) is picked per slot. Enchants and set bonuses are not counted.

How it works

+1 bonus healing: for every heal that did not overheal, how much one more point would have added (the spell's coefficient from the Forever client data, times your talent multiplier measured from your own heals, times the downrank penalty). Crits count 1.5×. Heals that overhealed gain nothing.

+1% crit: a non-crit that didn't overheal would have gained up to half its size, capped at the target's missing health; a crit that partly overhealed gains only the part of the crit bonus that landed. Water Shield / Illumination mana from crits counts in the mana-limited view. Crit rating = 14 per 1%.

Intellect: its crit, plus (mana-limited) 15 mana per point every time you drained your mana from full to under 10%.

Spirit / MP5: only count while you were below full mana (regen is wasted at full), split into inside / outside the five-second rule. Druid Bloom heals for your Spirit, so Druid Spirit has throughput value.

Throughput treats mana as free (you never ran out). Mana-limited values each point of mana at your healing per mana across all boss fights. Use mana-limited when you ran dry; rows where your mana never went below 40% are dimmed in that view.

Real mana costs: the log shows each spell's cost before talents, so the real cost is measured from your mana bar right after each cast.
