# Forever Healer Stat Weights

Stat weights for **WoW Forever** healers (Priest, Paladin, Shaman, Druid), worked out from your own combat log,
for every boss encounter in it.

**Use it:** open the GitHub Pages site for this repository, then drop your `WoWCombatLog-*.txt` on it.
The log is read by your own browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

**Offline:** download the repository and open `docs/index.html`. It works straight from disk.

## What you get

- Weights relative to +1 bonus healing for +1% crit, Intellect, Spirit and MP5. They come for the whole log,
  all boss fights together, trash, and each encounter.
- Two views:
  - **Throughput**: mana is free. This tells you what makes your heals bigger.
  - **Mana-limited**: each point of mana is worth your healing per mana in boss fights.
- **Boss fights of a set length (2, 3 and 6 min, estimates)**: your boss-fight weights carried over to a longer mana-limited
  fight. Mana for the fight = max mana + regen over the fight + potions (editable), so 1 mana is worth
  (healing / healing per +1 bonus healing) / (mana for the fight). Intellect counts its full 15 mana, Spirit and MP5 their regen
  over the fight. "Funded" compares that mana with your logged boss-fight pace: at 100% or more, use the Throughput weights.
- A per-spell table (casts, real mana, healing, overheal, crit rate, healing per mana) for any fight or all of them.
- A CSV export and a one-line "Copy summary" for Discord.
- **Best in bags**: paste the text from the SimulationCraft addon (`/simc`, current version; it includes the
  bank once you have opened it with the addon loaded), or the export from the voidsim.io or MythicSim Forever addon, and the page picks the best gear you
  own for your weights, slot by slot. It handles rings, trinkets, and two-handers against main hand plus off hand, and
  checks class armour and weapon types. Item stats are in `docs/items.js` (generated by `tools/gen_items.py`). Set
  pieces you own are swapped in where the set bonus is worth more than what they replace. Enchants are not counted.
- **Best in slot (every item)**: the top items in the game for your weights, per slot, with where each comes from. Classic
  raid loot is off by default, because Forever's raids are not the Classic ones.
- **Set bonuses**: each set's best number of pieces against the best-in-slot picks, the bonuses that gets you and the net
  change. Stat bonuses come from the client's `ItemSetSpell` table; proc and spell bonuses are modelled by hand in
  `tools/gen_procs.py` (`SET_SPEC`). Set membership is the client's, or wowtbc.gg's `set_id` for Forever's reworked
  Tier 0.5 pieces. Proc chances the client doesn't hold are assumed and noted on the page.
- **On-use and proc effects**, estimated in +healing from your own log, and added to those items in Best in bags and Best
  in slot:
  - Covers trinkets, relics and other items, plus the Insight weapon enchant.
  - Uses your cast rate per second of casting, which spells you cast, the healing +1 healing adds per cast, your crit
    value per spell and your healing per mana.
  - Durations, cooldowns, proc chances and internal cooldowns come from the client (`docs/procs.js`, generated by
    `tools/gen_procs.py`; what each effect does for a healer is set by hand there).
  - On-use cooldowns are replayed over the pulls in your log: boss fights, plus trash (stretches of your healing outside
    them, split by gaps over 20 sec). The item is used as soon as it's ready in a pull, then on cooldown. Saved for boss
    fights (the default), trash doesn't use it up; or it can be used on every pull. How far into each boss pull it was
    ready is then applied to a fight of the chosen length.
  - Cast-time effects and Swiftmend cooldown effects are listed but not scored.
- **Enchants and consumables**, valued in +healing:
  - Mana items (potions, runes, Flask of Distilled Wisdom) are converted through your healing per mana in the
    mana-limited view.
  - Which items exist in Forever, and the food values: method.gg's Forever list (beta, 2 Nov 2026). All other values come
    from the game client (`docs/extras.js`).

**Warcraft Logs link** (the second tab): paste a `forever.warcraftlogs.com` report link and sign in to Warcraft
Logs once. The page then reads the report through the Warcraft Logs API, still in your browser.

## Setting up the Warcraft Logs option (once, for whoever hosts the page)

1. Go to https://www.warcraftlogs.com/api/clients/ and create a client:
   - Name: anything.
   - Redirect URL: the page's exact address, e.g. `https://daemeous.github.io/forever-heal-weights/`.
   - Tick **Public Client**. This uses PKCE, so there is no secret to keep.
2. Put the client ID in `docs/wcl-config.js` and push.

The API returns events already parsed, not raw log lines. `docs/wcl.js` converts them into the same events the
raw-log reader produces. `node tools/wcl_roundtrip.js <log> <Player-GUID>` checks that conversion against a raw log.
Checked against a live Forever report (26 Nov 2026): field layout, talents (`talentTree`), absorbs, and boss pulls (`dungeonencounterstart`/`end` events inside one whole-dungeon fight).

## Before you start

- Turn on **Advanced Combat Logging** (System > Network) and type `/combatlog` before the run.
  The log ends up in `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Logs\`.
- The inputs fill themselves in from the log:
  - **Talents** come from the log's COMBATANT_INFO lines: Int %, Spirit %, Spirit → healing, Int → healing,
    regen while casting, and healing % bonuses.
  - **Bonus healing** is worked out from your heals: average non-crit heal = talent multiplier × (base + coefficient ×
    bonus healing), solved per spell, then the median is taken. This is *effective* bonus healing, including Spirit or
    Intellect that talents turn into healing, so it can sit above the character sheet figure.
  - Every input can still be edited.

## Method (short)

- **+1 bonus healing**: summed over every heal that did not overheal. Each heal counts its spell's coefficient
  (Forever client data) times your talent multiplier, measured from your own non-crit heals, times the downrank
  penalty. Crits count at your measured crit size (below).
- **Crit size** is measured from your own heals (mean crit / mean non-crit), so talents such as Paladin Holy Power
  are included without needing to model them.
- **+1% crit**:
  - A non-crit that didn't overheal gains half its size, capped at the target's missing health.
  - A crit gains only the part of its crit bonus that wasn't overheal.
  - Illumination mana counts in the mana-limited view. Water Shield too, but only from the direct heals that didn't
    crit while a crit would have given a globe (shield has charges, 3.5 sec since the last globe, no globe from the
    same cast yet) - not all globes, since being hit makes globes too. The recast GCD is not charged.
  - Forever has no crit rating: gear gives crit as a percentage (0.5%, 1%, ...), so the weight is per +1% crit.
- **Intellect**: its crit (about 0.0168% per point), plus 15 mana per point for each time you drained your mana from
  full to under 10% (mana-limited view).
- **Spirit / MP5**: regen only counts while you were below full mana, split into inside and outside the
  five-second rule. Druid Bloom heals for your Spirit.
- **Real mana costs**: the log shows the cost before talents. The real cost is measured from your mana bar right
  after each cast.
- **Downranking (Forever rule, community-measured)**: a rank keeps its full coefficient for 17 levels after its
  learn level, then loses 5% per level. Logs show a floor of about 10%.

Assumptions you may want to check:
- Spirit regen is one formula for every class in Forever: min(Spirit/4, 6.25 + Spirit/8) mana per second (0.125 per Spirit above 50), checked in game on Priest, Druid, Paladin and Shaman.
- Swiftmend gets bonus-healing credit as a full Rejuvenation. Earth Shield gets none.
- Shield absorbs (Power Word: Shield, Divine Aegis) count as healing done, with no bonus-healing credit.
- Only class healing spells count; potions and trinket heals are left out.

## Command line

```
node tools/cli.js WoWCombatLog-112426_134059.txt --player Name --bonus 450 [--json out.json]
```

## Updating the spell and talent data

`docs/spells.js` is generated by `tools/gen_spells.py` from the Forever client DB2 tables (wago.tools, product `wowf`).
`docs/talents.js` is generated by `tools/gen_talents.py`. Entry → talent comes from the client trait tables, and
per-rank values from the wowforevertalents.com descriptions, because the client's own talent values are inconsistent.
`docs/procs.js` is generated by `tools/gen_procs.py`; run it before `tools/gen_items.py`, which keeps the proc items even
when they have no stats. All of these scripts need the data cache from the parent workbook project. After regenerating, bump the `?v=` on the script tags
in `docs/index.html`.
